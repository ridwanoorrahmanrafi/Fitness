1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW
================================================================================
ARISE AI is an offline-first, modern athletic progressive web application (PWA) 
engineered for personalized workout tracking, AI-assisted routine generation, 
biomechanical movement guidance, and authentic Bangladeshi sports nutrition.

Key Architecture Characteristics:
- Pure Athletic Orientation: 100% free of fantasy RPG, "Solo Leveling", hunters,
  or gamified gates. Clean, professional sports science and athlete biometrics.
- Offline-First PWA: Full local execution via service worker, web manifest, 
  and resilient LocalStorage fallback with automatic cloud synchronization.
- High-Performance Audio Engine: Zero external MP3/WAV assets; pure Web Audio 
  API synthesizer generating crisp frequency-modulated feedback for sets, rest 
  timers, and workout completions.
- Dual-Mode Viewport: Sleek, responsive touch-first phone viewport frame on desktop 
  screens and full-bleed immersive experience on mobile devices.
- Bilingual Localization: Complete, simultaneous support for English and Bengali (বাংলা).
